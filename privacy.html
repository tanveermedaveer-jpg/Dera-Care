Privacy Policy

256-Bit Data Encryption Standards • Updated August 2026

1 Data We Collect

Dera Care collects minimal essential user data required for patient management: full name, email address, contact phone numbers, and optional medical notes submitted during appointment requests.

2 How Information Is Used

Your data is used strictly for authenticating patient sessions, facilitating appointment bookings with registered specialist doctors, and maintaining user preferences. We NEVER sell or commercialize patient records.

3 Data Security & Storage

All credentials and local application data are protected using industry-standard 256-bit encryption. Local storage records remain strictly private on user devices and authorized administrative channels.

4 Third-Party Services

When initiating WhatsApp booking communications, the app utilizes secure protocol deep-linking. Third-party messaging platforms process data in accordance with their respective privacy standards.

5 Contact Privacy Officer

If you have questions about our data practices or wish to request data deletion, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@deracare.pk.
